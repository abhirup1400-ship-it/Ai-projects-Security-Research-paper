# Ai-projects-Security-Research-paper

🚨 You built your website or app. But someone(Hacker) else may be looking for a way in. Can you keep it safe or pay debts?
My new research on AI-assisted application security........... Just read it before making projects for hackathons and others 

I’ve seen many friends and students building websites and applications for hackathons, Google Developer Group events, college projects, and other tech events. 🚀
With AI, building has become faster than ever.
But while we focus on making the project work, we often forget to ask:
Can someone misuse my API, credentials, database, or information? 🔐
One small security mistake can become a much bigger problem — sometimes even an unexpected financial burden. 💸
That’s why I researched this topic in my paper:
📄 “Built Fast, Broken Easy”
If you’re building with AI, sharing your project publicly, or preparing for your next hackathon, take a few minutes to understand what you might be exposing.
Build fast. Think secure. Stay safe. 🔒
